Data & Method

NASA data used now

  • NASA POWER: monthly max temperature (T2M_MAX), precipitation (PRECTOTCORR), root-zone soil wetness (GWETROOT), 2020-2025, one point per location.
  • NASA MODIS MOD13Q1 v061 (via AppEEARS): 16-day NDVI at 250 m for both points, Dec 2019 to Oct 2026 (good and marginal quality pixels; annual means use full years 2020-2025).
  • NASA GIBS: Blue Marble base map and MODIS/Terra NDVI 8-day vegetation imagery (about 250 m).

Crop fit rule (prototype)

Each crop has a water-need class and a heat-tolerance class (low / moderate / high) taken from general crop knowledge, not from NASA. Local water supply is high when the stress index is 0-2, moderate for 3-4, low for 5-6. Local heat stress is low, moderate or high when months above 40 °C are up to 5%, up to 30%, or more. Mismatch points = how far the crop's water need exceeds local supply + how far local heat stress exceeds its tolerance. 0 = good fit, 1 = fair, 2+ = poor.

Roadmap

  • Longer NDVI history and field-scale NDVI (Sentinel-2 / Landsat)
  • SMAP soil moisture and ECOSTRESS surface temperature
  • Crop suitability layers (FAO GAEZ) and local soil data
  • Climate scenario sliders and an open-question assistant

NASA POWER points are approximate (Jazan 17.015, 42.645; Qassim 26.196, 43.738). NASA does not endorse this project.
